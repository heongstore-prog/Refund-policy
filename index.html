Refund Policy

Heong Arena | Last Updated: August 21, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how refunds for paid tournament registrations on Heong Arena are handled.

1. General Refund Policy

Tournament entry fees are generally non-refundable once a successful tournament registration has been completed, except where a refund is approved under this policy or required by applicable law.

Please review the tournament details and rules carefully before making a payment.

2. Failed Payments

If a payment fails and no successful payment is received, no successful tournament registration will be created.

If your account or bank shows that money was deducted even though the payment was unsuccessful, the transaction may be reviewed based on the payment provider's transaction status.

3. Duplicate Payments

If you believe you have been charged more than once for the same tournament registration, contact Heong Arena support.

We will review the transaction details and, if a duplicate payment is confirmed, the eligible duplicate amount may be refunded.

4. Tournament Cancellation

If Heong Arena cancels a tournament after a participant has successfully paid and registered, the participant may be eligible for a refund of the tournament entry fee.

Refund eligibility and processing will depend on the circumstances of the cancellation and applicable law.

5. Tournament Postponement

If a tournament is postponed and a new date is announced, an existing registration may remain valid for the rescheduled tournament.

If a different refund arrangement applies, affected participants will be informed where appropriate.

6. User Cancellation

If a participant voluntarily decides not to participate after successfully registering for a tournament, the entry fee is generally non-refundable unless otherwise stated for that particular tournament or required by applicable law.

7. Disqualification

Tournament entry fees are generally non-refundable when a participant is disqualified because of:

  • Cheating
  • Violation of tournament rules
  • Fraudulent activity
  • Providing false information
  • Unauthorized software or tools
  • Manipulation of tournament systems
  • Other prohibited activities

8. Fraudulent or Unauthorized Transactions

Transactions suspected of fraud, abuse, manipulation, or unauthorized activity may be investigated before a refund is processed. Additional verification may be required where appropriate.

9. Refund Request

For refund-related questions or requests, contact: heongbuisnesspvt.ltd@gmail.com

Please include:

  • Username
  • Tournament name
  • Tournament ID
  • Razorpay Order ID, if available
  • Razorpay Payment ID, if available
  • Amount paid
  • Date of payment
  • Reason for the refund request
Important: Do not send your password, UPI PIN, CVV, OTP, or other sensitive security information.

10. Refund Processing

Once a refund is approved, it will normally be processed through the applicable payment channel or original payment method where possible.

The time required for the refund to appear in your account may depend on the payment provider, bank, card network, or other financial institution.

11. Refund Review

Submitting a refund request does not guarantee that a refund will be approved. Each request may be reviewed based on the transaction details, tournament status, payment status, applicable rules, and applicable law.

12. Policy Changes

Heong Arena may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on the Heong Arena platform and will become effective when posted, unless otherwise stated.
